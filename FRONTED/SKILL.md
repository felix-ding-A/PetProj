## Mandatory 4-Step Editorial Workflow

### Step 1 — Topic & Keyword Anti-Cannibalization Check
1. Read the requested article entry from `FRONTED/NovaPetNutra_100篇行业洞察SEO内容规划.md`.
2. Ensure the article's `primaryKeyword` and `slug` do not collide with any static Money Page (`/` = `pet supplement contract manufacturer`, `/our-facility` = `pet supplement factory`, `/chewable-tablet-manufacturing`, `/capsule-manufacturing`, `/powder-manufacturing`, `/drops-manufacturing`, `/paste-manufacturing`).
3. Ensure every title and slug explicitly carries a pet/animal modifier (`Pet`, `Dog`, `Cat`, `Feline`, `Canine`, `Equine`, `Poultry`) so zero pages collide with human nutraceuticals.

### Step 2 — Content Brief & Internal Link Blueprint
Before writing, plan:
- **Target Buyer Persona** (Brand Founder / Procurement Manager / Amazon FBA Seller / R&D Lead)
- **GEO Opening Answer** (40–60 words directly answering the core query for ChatGPT / Perplexity / Google AI Overviews)
- **H2 / H3 Outline** (never use `# H1` inside Markdown body)
- **Structured Comparison Table** (at least 1 Markdown table per article; 2–3 tables for Whitepapers)
- **Internal Link Equity (3–6 links to real site routes)**:
  1. Early body → Primary Money Page (e.g., `/`, `/our-facility`, or the matching `/<dosage>-manufacturing` page)
  2. Mid body → Relevant `/private-label`, `/our-process`, or `/quality-compliance` page
  3. Closing CTA → `/contact-us`

### Step 3 — Draft B2B Article (Sanity-Ready)
Produce clean, Sanity-ready output including:
- **Sanity Metadata**: `Title`, `Slug` (lowercase hyphenated), `Category`, `Author` (`NovaPet Nutra Formulation Team`), `Meta Title` (≤ 62 chars), `Meta Description` (≤ 159 chars), `Excerpt` (≤ 240 chars), `Related Dosage Forms`.
- **Markdown Body**:
  - Crisp, practical B2B factory English (zero AI clichés: `seamless`, `effortless`, `delve`, `tapestry`, `unleash`, `game-changer`, `cutting-edge`, `next-generation`, `zero guesswork`, `peace of mind`).
  - Concrete manufacturing parameters (`<38°C`, `Aw < 0.60`, `<35% RH`, `±3%` fill tolerance, `500-unit` pilot MOQ, `18–28 days`, `ISO/IEC 17025 COA`, `ISTA` transit testing) without inventing fake statistics or fake third-party surveys.
  - Target length: **1,000–1,500+ words** for standard insights; **2,000–3,000 words** for Industry Whitepapers (`#75–#86`).
  - Include 2–3 `[Sanity Editor Image Note]` slots with recommended Alt Text for real factory/product photos.

### Step 4 — 20-Point E-E-A-T & Compliance Self-Audit
Before delivering the article to the user, verify **0 hard compliance violations** and score **≥ 16/20**:
- **Hard Redlines (0 tolerance)**:
  - No medical/disease/veterinary-drug claims (`cures`, `treats arthritis/cancer/infection`, `antibiotic`, `lymphoma`, `lipoma`, `anti-vomiting`, `constipation relief`).
  - Never write `FDA approved` or `AAFCO certified`.
  - No `# H1` in Markdown body, no fabricated statistics, no fictional author personas.
- **20-Point E-E-A-T Scorecard**:
  - **Experience (0–5)**: ≥3 concrete production parameters + ≥1 real manufacturing/shipping pitfall & fix + photo placement notes.
  - **Expertise (0–5)**: ≥1 decision comparison table + accurate B2B terminology + real team attribution.
  - **Authoritativeness (0–5)**: Accurate references to real standards (`cGMP`, `ISO 22000`, `HACCP`, `ISO/IEC 17025`, `FDA CVM`, `AAFCO`, `NASC`, `EU Regulation (EC) No 767/2009`, `ISTA`) + proper internal links to Money Pages.
  - **Trustworthiness (0–5)**: 0 medical claims, realistic MOQ/lead-time ranges, aligned with `Shaanxi Ginkvora Biotechnology Co., Ltd.` (`D-U-N-S®: 975262249`).
- **Deliver with**:
  1. E-E-A-T score breakdown (`X/20`)
  2. **《老板 30 秒事实核对清单》** listing all numbers, MOQs, lead times, standards, and internal links so the founder can verify accuracy in 30 seconds before pasting into Sanity Studio.
